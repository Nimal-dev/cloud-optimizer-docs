Stop paying for servers that do nothing

Companies rent hundreds of cloud servers and pay for every hour. Many sit almost idle. The Cloud Resource Optimizer predicts each server's usage for the next 4 hours and tells you which ones to keep, shrink or shut down.

How it works in three steps

1. Monitor

Read each server's CPU and memory usage, recorded every 5 minutes.

2. Predict

AI models forecast the next 4 hours: a likely value (P50) and a safety limit (P90).

3. Recommend

Simple rules turn the forecast into an action: keep, right-size or shut down.

How accurate is the AI?
